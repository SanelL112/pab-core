#### Solutions
- **Word Problem 1:**
  - 70 miles in 2 hours = 35 miles per hour.
  - Total time = 2 hours + 3 hours = 5 hours.

- **Word Problem 2:**
  - 5 rows of corn * 8 rows of beans = 40 corns and beans planted in total.

#### Practice Problems
- **Geometry Problems**
  - **1. A rectangle has a length of 6 meters and a width of 3 meters. What is the area?**
  - **2. The perimeter of a square is 24 inches. How long is each side?**

#### Solutions
- **Geometry Problem 1:**
  - Area = Length * Width = 6 meters * 3 meters = 18 square meters.

- **Geometry Problem 2:**
  - Side length = Perimeter / 4 = 24 inches / 4 = 6 inches.
