FRC game information

Teams must design, fabricate, assemble, wire, and program a robot to play the Crescendo game
Teams are given a kit with the base electronics and motors, and a base “kitbot”
Our team has elected to build the kitbot and modify it to meet our strategy

FRC information
Teams will also get judged at competitions and can win awards for both technical aspects of the robot as well as business and culture aspects of the program
Several teams bring giveaways and buttons to help promote their program and for fun.
There are a lot of elements to FRC, not just the robot!
