Career Tech Student Organization (CTSO) aimed at promoting STEM in the school and community

Vex Robotics
Develop technical skills, leadership, and cross-discipline teamwork

Introduction
Lambert Robotics
Fall meetings:
Mondays and Wednesdays 3:50-6:30
Occasional Friday or Saturday when approaching competition
Spring meetings:
Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays 3:50 to 7:00
Saturdays 9:30 to 1:00pm
May meet later or more often as needed to prepare for competition
Dues
$415 on School Pay ($400 if by check - payable to Lambert Robotics)
Covers robot parts, T-shirt costs, some of travel

Lambert Robotics Competition Teams
Lambert Robotics will compete in the following:
FIRST Robotics Competition
Primarily in Spring Semester
~45 team members, 1 robot about the size of a fridge
Requires custom designs, manufacturing, electronics, and programming
Played in 3 vs. 3 matches on a basketball court-size field
This is considered our Varsity program
Vex Robotics Competition
Small robots built from kits; 6-7 kids per team
Fall to early spring semester
Played in 2 vs. 2 matches

Lambert Robotics Competition Teams
Vex Robotics Competition
Vex State qualifier events
West Forsyth Qualifier - October 7
TSA FLC - Jekyll Island November 10-12
SFHS Over and Under - December 16 (South Forsyth)
Vex State Tournament (Must qualify)
Early February 2024
Vex Worlds (Must Qualify)
April 23-May 2 in Dallas, Tx
Vex is great for students who want to work with their friends on a small robot
