LHS Investment Club

The Lambert Investment Club

Vision & Purpose

To educate members about personal finance, investing, and the fundamentals of wealth building. The club provides hands-on experience through stock market simulations, investment research, and financial literacy activities, preparing students to make informed financial decisions in the future.

	

Events & Activities:

Monthly and bi-weekly investment education & financial curriculum.
Influential individuals among the local community as guest speakers.
Second semester paper trading
stock investment competition.
Potential field trips to travel to financial institutions in the area.

Meeting Information

Monday's from 7:45 AM - 8:15 AM in Room 1232 at end of 1200 hallway.

Our Monthly Club Meeting is the first Monday of every month. 

Second Monday of every month is meeting for beginner level students.

Third Monday of every month is meeting for intermediate level students.

	

Participation Guidelines

Open to all Lambert High School students interested in finance and investing. Signup permission form is required as well as Member Interest Form submission. No prior experience required. Members are encouraged to actively participate in meetings and events.





	

Other Information

Members have opportunities to compete in investment competitions, attend guest speaker events, collaborate with local business leaders, and even participate in field trips around the county.
