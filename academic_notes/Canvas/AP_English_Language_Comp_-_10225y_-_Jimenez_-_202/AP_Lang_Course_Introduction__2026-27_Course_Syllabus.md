	January

	February
	Inaugural Speeches by John F. Kennedy and Ronald Reagan along with letters and op-eds
 
Writing:  Synthesis Essay
	The Power of Beauty:  Visual Rhetoric
	March
	The Great Gatsby by F. Scott Fitzgerald
Visual work by Norman Rockwell, Annie Leibovitz, and editoria
