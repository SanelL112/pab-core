Physics Team - Trip to CalTech for USAYPT
We are taking a team of 12 students to CalTech for the USAYPT. Four of our teammates will be presenting the experimental design, results and conclusions for the 2027 questions. We have 8 spots for "scouts". “Scouts” are additional school members who may watch any of the rounds happening throughout the tournament. Schools can bring up to 8 scouts. As is the case for all audience members to a Physics Fight, scouts are not allowed to take any notes, make any recordings, or communicate in any way with people outside of the round. As soon as they leave the room at the end of the round, they may speak to their teammates and write things down - but it must be from memory. This means your scouts must also understand the concepts discussed in the problems, so it is a good idea to have scouts who have strong familiarity with the problems and have worked on them closely with the team members serving as Reporters! The cost of the trip is approximately $1,100. This is roundtrip flight to Pasadena, hotel, transportation and 5 meals at the event. If you are interested in this opportunity, please complete the form below. https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1RxHiYQcHSnY75mYqCxR2NTLIiFD3FOJ-KwE1TwEiw3I/edit
