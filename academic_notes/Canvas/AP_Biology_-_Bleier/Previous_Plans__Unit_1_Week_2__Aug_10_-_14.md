Success Criteria

 

	

By the end of the week students should be able to:

Explain what is required for two unbonded atoms to form a hydrogen bond
Identify two water properties, why they are important for life, and how water's molecular structure explains the properties
Identify atoms, important subregions, monomers, and functions of the four types of macromolecules that make up all living cells
