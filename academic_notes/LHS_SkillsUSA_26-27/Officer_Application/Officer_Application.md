# Officer Application
**Module:** Officer Application

One of the pillars of SkillsUSA is leadership and Lambert SkillsUSA is committed to providing that opportunity for all members. If you are interested in becoming a Officer for our chapter this year, please fill out the application below. Officers will work directly with other officers and advisors to organize events, assist with competition goals, and support members who have questions. Anyone can apply.
