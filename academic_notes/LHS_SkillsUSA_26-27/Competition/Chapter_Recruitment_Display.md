# Chapter Recruitment Display
**Module:** Competition

(Team of 3) SkillsUSA student members build a three-dimensional display that articulates the annual SkillsUSA competition theme. The members of the chapter build the display and three students present information about the display during a presentation and interview with judges.

 

The SkillsUSA competition theme for the 2026-27 school year is:

 

SkillsUSA: Level UP
The topic to be addressed by competitors in the 2027 SkillsUSA Chapter Display, Prepared Speech and Promotional Bulletin Board competitions.

The theme is designed to inspire the amazing work in our SkillsUSA chapters during the school year as students develop the Personal, Workplace and Technical Skills of our SkillsUSA Framework.
