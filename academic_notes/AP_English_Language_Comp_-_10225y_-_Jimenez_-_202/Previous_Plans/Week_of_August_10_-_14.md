Success Criteria

 

By the end of the week, students should be able to:

identify and explain the key components of an argument, including claims, reasons, evidence, and warrants.

distinguish between different types of evidence used to support an argument (such as facts, statistics, expert testimony, and anecdotes).

analyze and construct a clear line of reasoning that logically connects claims, reasons, and evidence.

recognize and write effective counterarguments and rebuttals to strengthen their own arguments.

evaluate the use of context and subtext in argumentative texts to understand deeper meanings and persuasive techniques
