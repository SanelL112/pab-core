Summative assessments identify standards where students demonstrate or lack proficiency. Educators use assessment evidence to accurately report levels of student proficiency in a grade-level subject or course and guide next steps. 	Academic Integrity
	It is the expectation of Forsyth County Schools that students exhibit academic integrity when submitting evidence of learning. When students submit evidence that does not authentically represent what they know and can do, the evidence is rendered invalid. Examples of academic dishonesty are outlined in both the Family Engagement Guide & Student Handbook and The Forsyth County Schools Code of Conduct. Depending on the severity of the situation, academic dishonesty/cheating may result in the loss of credit, completing an alternative assignment and/or disciplinary consequences up to a tribunal referral.


LHS HONOR CODE
The Lambert High School honor code is aligned with Longhorn Pride. This means that Longhorns demonstrate high integrity and are resilient in their academic pursuits.
An essential part of education is developing a sense of honor, responsibility, and ethical principles that extend to all facets of life. Our students earn respect for themselves and their academic work through academic integrity and ethical behavior. Cheating is inexcusable conduct and will be dealt with strictly to preserve the integrity of the learning environment and assessment practices of Lambert High School. 
FCS Code of Conduct States: Academic dishonesty, cheating, plagiarism, altering record, or other fraudulent act on school assignments, exams, records, or college entrance examinations; using the computer network for any illegal activity such as copying or downloading copyrighted software, music, images or violation of copyright laws which includes using BYOT devices to copy or share copyrighted items or intellectual property. (Academic dishonesty/cheating may result in the loss of credit, completing an alternative assignment and/or disciplinary consequences up to a tribunal referral). (DM32) 
In addition to disciplinary consequences, the instance(s) may be included in any college reference questionnaires or letters or could result in a loss of privileges like officer positions or honor society membership.
	Consequences for High School Students:
1st Offense - Parent contact, academic integrity contract, retake with loss of credit up to 40% (max score 60)
2nd Offense - Parent contact, loss of credit up to 100%, removal from honor clubs/societies, possible in-school or out-of-school suspension
3rd Offense - Parent contact, loss of credit up to 100%, possible out-of-school suspension
Standardized Assessments - Any suspicion or instances of cheating on standardized tests will be reported to the governing agency and, based on the severity of the behavior, will receive disciplinary consequences
Severe Offenses -  Should a student engage in behavior that compromises the integrity of an assessment, regardless of whether it is a first offense - and particularly when it affects multiple students of staff (i.e. theft of assessment, pictures/dissemination of content, organized efforts to cheat or help others cheat, etc.), immediate and more severe consequences (up to a disciplinary tribunal) will be applied. 
*Students who face consequences for submitting work that does not reflect their own knowledge and skills may only reassess in accordance with the FCS Academic Integrity Consequences for High School Students outlined above. 
	

Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence
Artificial Intelligence refers to computer systems that are trained with data to accomplish tasks normally requiring human intelligence. AI is used across industries and communities and can serve as a helpful tool in educational settings. Students should learn how to evaluate if and when they should use AI and develop responsible and ethical practices when using AI tools. Students with Forsyth County Schools are expected to uphold the highest standards of academic integrity. This includes the responsible use of emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence.


Students will:
* Use approved age-appropriate AI tools for educational purposes only
* Follow teacher guidance on the use of AI tools
* Submit work that is fundamentally their own and provide proper citations when AI is used
* Evaluate the purpose, safety, and the trustworthiness of AI tools before use
* Review and critically assess output of AI tools recognizing that AI may contain bias and inaccuracies
* Not use AI tools to propagate harmful, misleading, offensive or inappropriate content 


All members of Forsyth County Schools’ community agree to follow the Forsyth County Schools Code of Conduct, school rules and commit to the following responsible use guidelines.


FCS High School Distraction-Free Learning Information
	FCS High School Cell Phone/ Electronic Device Contract In alignment with GA HB340, the Distraction-Free Education Act, which mandates that local school systems establish policies governing personal electronic device use for students in kindergarten through twelfth grade. Under this policy, high school students’ personal electronic devices must remain secured and out of sight from 8:25 AM until the end of the school day at 3:40 PM, except for permitted use during lunch periods.


CELL PHONE EXPECTATION
It is the expectation that all Forsyth County students silence and put away all electronic devices (cell phones, smart watches, personal laptops, ipads, etc.) during the school day with the exception of the students’ assigned lunch period. 
	“Personal electronic device” means any portable electronic device capable of transmitting, receiving, or accessing communications, data, or media. This term includes, but is not limited to personal laptops and headphones, smartphones, smartwatches, tablets, e-readers, smart glasses and other devices with functionalities such as wireless communication, internet access, messaging, video recording, gaming, social media access, or data transmission.
