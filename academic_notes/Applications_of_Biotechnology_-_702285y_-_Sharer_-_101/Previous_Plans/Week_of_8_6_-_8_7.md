Success Criteria

 

By the end of the week/this unit students will be able to...

HS-AB-5

Incorporate required safety practices and procedures in performing tasks encountered in the laboratory setting.

HS-AB-5.2

Analyze case studies of lab accidents and biohazards in various settings (i.e., include dangers of gases, explosions, electrical shock, biohazards, infectious disease, and genetically modified organisms; also other topics as suggested by current events).

HS-AB-5.3

Demonstrate ways to prevent or manage lab accidents and biohazards in various workplace settings.

 

HS-AB-6

Assess current trends, ethical, legal, and regulatory issues related to the development of biotechnology products. Demonstrate CPR, First Aid, and the AED utilizing current standards.

HS-AB-6.1

Monitor scientific journals, Internet sources, mass media, and industry associations to identify current trends and policy issues in biotechnology.

HS-AB-6.2

Distinguish between marketing material and experimentally validated information.

HS-AB-6.3

Describe the concept of integrity and the ethical use of statistics, controls, and standards.

HS-AB-6.4

Demonstrate bioethical/legal issues to various scenarios, including clinical trials, Institutional Review Boards (IRB) applications, privacy (HIPAA), choice of genetic traits, and use of genetic testing data.

HS-AB-6.5

Describe intellectual property rights, technology transfer, and how biotechnology is funded.

HS-AB-6.6

Explain the meaning of human dignity and informed consent in biotechnology and healthcare.

HS-AB-6.7

Describe the role of federal regulatory agencies and the Code of Federal Regulations applicable to biotechnology (e.g., FDA [Food and Drug Administration], 21 CFR [Code of Federal Regulations], EPA [Environmental Protection Agency], NIH [National Institute of Health], USDA [United States Department of Agriculture], etc.) and the relationship to international regulatory systems (e.g., ICH, etc.).

HS-AB-6.8

Explain the phases of clinical trials and requirements for obtaining FDA product approval.

HS-AB-6.9

Define the purpose of quality assurance, quality control, method validation, documentation, current Good Manufacturing Practices and Good Laboratory Practices.

HS-AB-6.10

Document and keep accurate records according to regulatory requirements.

What is iGEM?
iGEM (International Genetically Engineered Machine Competition)  is a collegiate competition in the field of synthetic biology.

Students use biobricks (DNA sequences) to turn cells into factories that produce a desired product or build biological circuits.

iGEM teams compete for medals and special prizes during the Grand Jamboree by presenting and defending their work.

What is a Proposal? 

Write a proposal for the 2025 iGEM continuation project. 
You must include at least one wet-lab goal, one human practices goal, as well as suggesting one other aspect of the project including hardware, software, human practices, educational outreach or modeling. 
Your project proposal should show clear and relevant goals for the 2025 season. 
Include in-text and end-text citations in APA format. 
Your proposal should be double spaced and 2-3 pages long excluding cover page and citation page(s). 
 PDF link only as your submission.
Wetlab - uses parts registry  parts and proposes a novel idea.  Could be a:
New part
New use of an existing part
New combination of parts
Other aspect - suggest a novel hardware device, or software approach to supplement the project.



Designing Your BioBrick Construct
What is a BioBrick? 
BioBricks, or biological parts, can be thought as building blocks or legos that can be put together in different ways to assemble larger synthetic biological circuits that together have a unique function.

So what should a plasmid insert include?
Promoters
RBS (Ribosomal Binding Sites)
Gene of Interest
Reporter Proteins
Terminators

You can find many existing biobricks on the iGEM Parts Registry, and choose the parts that you believe would fit well with your wetlab proposal. Specifically for your gene of interest, if it doesn’t exist already on the Parts Registry, feel free to propose a new part with the DNA sequence (if you can find one)!
