Maintaining a Lab Notebook:
Used to document the experiment being performed.
Why is this necessary?




This is a permanent record. Use pen in a bound book, it stays in the lab at all times for future reference. 

Transferring new inventions from the research lab to manufacturing is not possible if the procedures and observations are not well documented.
‘Commit nothing to memory, write everything down.’

Lab Notebook Set Up: FDA Guidelines

Front cover, you should write your name, the course name, and the year in black Sharpie.​
ONLY BLUE OR BLACK PENS SHOULD BE USED! ABSOLUTELY NO PENCIL!​
If you make a mistake, do not use white out. Scratch through it with a single line.​
Whoops! I made a mistake!​
Step 1: Label pages 1-4 as Table of Contents.​
Step 2: Each page after the Table of Contents needs to be numbered in the top corner (front and back). The back of each page is used for random notes and scratch work.


Components of a Lab Notebook Entry:
Title - Clearly identifies the experiment or protocol
Hypothesis - Only if needed (not for making buffers ect)
Materials - All specialized equipment including model/serial number if appropriate
Procedures or Methods - Document each action completed, all details, included calculations
Results - Detailed observations, sketches and graphs, clearly labeled
Conclusion - Discuss how results relate to experiment purpose, ideas for future investigations, summarize references that relate

Who Can Join?
Students may apply to join NTHS in beginning of Junior Year*
Must be enrolled in 2nd or 3rd level CTAE Pathway course
Must be Active member of CTSO
Must have and maintain a 3.5 GPA
All completed CTAE courses earned final grade of 85% or higher

Member Expectations
To Complete the NTHS Requirements and Graduate with NTHS Honors members must:
CTSO Membership in both Junior AND Senior year
Complete 12 service credits for their CTSO by Spring of Senior Year
*Any students who have not completed at least 5 service credits by end of Junior year will be removed from NTHS

FAQ’s
What are “Service Credits”?  Speak with your CTSO Adviser or Officer Team.  Types of credits can vary from club to club, but often include credits for Community Service and Drives, Competition, Conference Attendance, and participation in CTSO specific programs or activities.
Can I join as a Senior?  This approval can be made at discretion of your CTSO’s NTHS  Adviser - BUT ALL REQUIREMENTS MUST STILL BE COMPLETED
Can I join as a Sophomore?  No, new applicants must apply at beginning of their Junior year.
Do all my credits have to be completed with the same CTSO? YES! The CTSO that you choose in your application is the CTSO that you will have to complete credits with
I take my CTAE courses virtually, can I join? Yes! But be mindful that it is up to YOU to make sure you are meeting requirements and completing service credits for your CTSO.  
What happens if I get behind on requirements? Students should strive to have approx. 8 of the Service Credits completed Junior year. Anyone who has not completed a minimum of 5 service credits by end of Junior year may be removed from NTHS.
