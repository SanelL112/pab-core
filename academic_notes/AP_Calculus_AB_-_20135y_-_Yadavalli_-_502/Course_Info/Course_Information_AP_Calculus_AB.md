### Linked Google Doc: Student Google account
﻿Having trouble accessing itslearning or Classlink resources?


Please go through the following steps to ensure you are logged into your school Google account, and not a personal account:
1. Make sure you are using Chrome as your browser (not Safari).
2. Clear browsing history/data/cache. (upper right hand corner, three dots, click on “settings”, “privacy and security” listed to the left, then choose “Clear browsing data”)
3. Go to the Lambert High School Home Page and click on MYFCS itslearning icon  at the top of the page.
4.  Log into Classlink using (student# @forsythk12.org).
5. Then try accessing itslearning or other app.
*IF in the upper right corner of Google, you do not see a circle with your initials, click on it and you can see if you are logged in or not. If it is a grayed out person or another person’s initials, you may need to sign-out and/or restart and log in again.
