# Intro to lab safety section
**Module:** Learn More: Course resources

In this section of the course orientation, you will review the basics of lab safety and complete a short safety quiz.

You are welcome to download the pdf and even reference it while you take the quiz – for us, safety is a practice, not just getting correct quiz answers.

The quiz is not graded, but you need to score 100% to do labs in this course. You have unlimited attempts.

Do not worry about printing and signing the signature part of the safety pdf – you will sign off for lab safety with the Google Form that is the last resource of this section.
