Success Criteria

 

By the end of the week students should be able to:

Reflect on your notetaking / study strategies based on your performance on the first midpoint quiz

Identify what regions are interacting and what chemical bonds are formed at each of the four levels of protein structure

Predict how protein structure and function might change when one amino acid is changed (depending on which amino acid is changed for which other and depending on where in the protein the change occurs)

Identify environmental factors that speed up, slow down, AND entirely stop enzyme activity

Explain why we should graph not only sample means but also 2 SEM error bars when presenting data to readers

Justify claims with evidence (including use of statistical analyses)

Describe how environmental factors like pH, temperature, enzyme and substrate concentrations affect enzyme speed

Explain at the molecular level why enzyme speed changed

Identify environmental factors that denature enzymes and why these factors break certain bonds in enzyme protein structure
